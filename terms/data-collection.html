How Voidshard may collect and use information for marketing, recruitment, events, user agreements, support and game operations.

TermsLicenseDataNDA

This Data Collection and Privacy Notice explains the categories of information Voidshard may receive when people interact with its websites, games, publishing activities, recruitment, events, playtests, marketing campaigns, support channels and agreements. The exact data collected depends on the service you choose to use.

1. Information you provide

Voidshard may receive information you submit directly, such as your name, contact details, age or age range where relevant, account or platform identifiers, support messages, survey responses, event registrations, mailing-list preferences, competition entries, applications, CVs, portfolios, work history, references, playtest feedback, signed agreements and other information included in forms or correspondence.

2. Game and service information

Where enabled for a particular title or service, Voidshard may receive technical and usage information such as device type, operating system, game version, crash reports, diagnostics, session information, feature interactions, approximate region, language, referral source, anti-abuse signals and platform-generated identifiers. Voidshard should collect only information reasonably connected to operating, securing, understanding or improving the relevant service.

3. Marketing

Marketing information may include email addresses, campaign engagement, mailing-list choices, event interests and similar preference data. Voidshard may use this information to send news, release information, event notices, promotional material or research invitations where permitted. Users should be given a practical way to opt out of direct marketing communications.

4. Recruitment and job opportunities

For job, internship, freelance or collaboration opportunities, Voidshard may process application details, portfolios, work samples, interview notes, availability, eligibility information and correspondence for the purpose of assessing candidates, arranging interviews, maintaining recruitment records and contacting suitable applicants about relevant opportunities.

5. Events, playtests and community programmes

For physical or online events, Voidshard may process registration details, attendance information, access requirements voluntarily provided by attendees, consent records, photography or recording permissions, playtest participation, feedback and conduct reports. Additional notices may apply where an event is operated with a venue, platform or partner.

6. Agreements and business records

Voidshard may retain information needed to manage licences, NDAs, publishing relationships, contributor arrangements, key distribution, press access, invoices, permissions, disputes, compliance records and other business relationships. This can include identity and contact information, agreement versions, signatures, dates, access records and relevant correspondence.

7. Why information is used

Information may be used to provide and secure services; respond to enquiries; operate games and online features; administer accounts, keys and agreements; recruit staff or contractors; organise events; conduct research and playtests; prevent abuse or fraud; maintain records; comply with legal obligations; protect Voidshard, its users and partners; and improve products, communications and business operations.

8. Sharing information

Voidshard may share information with service providers and partners that support hosting, analytics, communications, recruitment, ticketing, payment processing, platform services, security, professional advice or event delivery. Information may also be disclosed where required by law, to protect legal rights or safety, or as part of a legitimate business restructuring. Voidshard does not treat personal information as a product for sale.

9. Retention

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including appropriate periods for security, contractual records, recruitment administration, financial records, disputes and legal obligations. Retention periods may differ between categories of data.

10. Security

Voidshard should use reasonable organisational and technical safeguards appropriate to the information being handled. No online system can be guaranteed completely secure, and users should avoid sending highly sensitive information through ordinary contact channels unless requested through an appropriate process.

11. Your choices and requests

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances, individuals may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability or withdrawal of consent. Requests can be submitted through the Voidshard contact page and may require reasonable identity verification before information is disclosed or changed.

12. Children and younger users

Voidshard should not intentionally collect more information from children than is appropriate for the relevant service. Where parental or guardian consent is required, the applicable service should provide an appropriate process before collecting or using the relevant information.

13. Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated as Voidshard introduces new games, services, recruitment processes, events or business tools. The current version will display its latest update date at the top of this page.

This privacy notice is a general template. Actual data collection should be mapped against the services Voidshard really operates and reviewed for compliance with applicable privacy and data-protection law before publication.
