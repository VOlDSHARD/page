NON-DISCLOSURE
AGREEMENT.

Confidentiality terms covering Voidshard titles, prototypes, source materials, business information and unreleased content.

These Non-Disclosure Agreement terms ("NDA Terms") describe the confidentiality obligations that may apply when Voidshard gives a person or organisation access to non-public information relating to any Voidshard title, prototype, project, publishing activity, partner relationship or internal operation. Where a separate NDA is signed, that signed agreement controls if it conflicts with this page.

1. Confidential information

"Confidential Information" includes non-public information disclosed in any form, including unreleased or unannounced games, prototypes, builds, roadmaps, source code, project files, assets, gameplay systems, mechanics, designs, scripts, story material, characters, screenshots, footage, audio, documentation, credentials, technical information, security details, financial information, publishing plans, marketing plans, release information, partner information, contracts, internal communications and information that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential.

2. Studio-wide coverage

Unless Voidshard expressly limits an NDA to a named project, confidentiality obligations apply to protected information encountered across all Voidshard studio titles and internal projects. Access to one project does not grant permission to disclose information learned about another project.

3. Recipient obligations

The recipient must keep Confidential Information secure; use it only for the authorised purpose; not publish, stream, upload, copy, distribute, sell, leak or disclose it to unauthorised people; not share credentials or restricted builds; and take reasonable precautions to prevent accidental disclosure. Access should be limited to people who genuinely need the information and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as these NDA Terms.

4. Proprietary materials

Source code, repositories, project files, internal tools, development documentation, proprietary technology and unreleased assets remain the exclusive property of Voidshard or its licensors. Access is temporary and limited to the approved purpose. Nothing in these NDA Terms transfers ownership, grants a source licence or permits use in another product.

5. Excluded information

Confidential Information does not normally include information the recipient can demonstrate was already lawfully known without a duty of confidentiality, became public through no breach by the recipient, was lawfully received from an authorised third party without confidentiality restrictions, or was independently developed without using Voidshard Confidential Information.

6. Required disclosure

If the recipient is legally compelled to disclose Confidential Information, the recipient should, where legally permitted, notify Voidshard promptly and disclose only the minimum information legally required so that appropriate protective steps can be considered.

7. Embargoes, press and public discussion

An embargo date, review guide, press instruction or written approval defines when specified information may be made public. Until that permission takes effect, protected information must remain confidential. Public information about one aspect of a title does not automatically remove confidentiality from unreleased builds, source materials, internal discussions or other non-public details.

8. Security incidents and accidental disclosure

If Confidential Information is lost, leaked, accessed by an unauthorised person or accidentally published, the recipient must notify Voidshard as soon as reasonably possible, preserve relevant evidence and cooperate in reasonable steps to contain the disclosure.

9. Return, deletion and revoked access

On request, expiry of authorised access or termination of the relationship, the recipient must stop using Confidential Information and return or securely delete restricted copies, builds, project files, documents and credentials where reasonably requested, except for records that must be retained by law. Voidshard may revoke keys, repository access, accounts, credentials, physical materials or other access associated with a confidential programme.

10. Breach and remedies

Unauthorised disclosure of confidential or proprietary material can cause serious harm to a game, studio, partner or release plan. A breach may result in immediate removal from projects or programmes, termination of access or contractual relationships, revocation of keys and credentials, recovery of reasonably provable losses, injunctive or protective court relief, and any other remedies available under the applicable agreement or law. These consequences are remedies for breach and are not intended to create arbitrary penalties.

11. Duration

Confidentiality obligations continue for as long as the relevant information remains confidential or for any specific period stated in a signed NDA, project agreement or disclosure notice. Trade secrets and similarly protected proprietary information may remain protected for so long as they retain that protected status under applicable law.

12. No obligation to proceed

Disclosure of Confidential Information does not require Voidshard to offer employment, enter a publishing relationship, release a title, continue a project, provide further access or complete any proposed transaction unless a separate written agreement says otherwise.

13. Other agreements

These NDA Terms may operate alongside the Voidshard Terms of Service, licence terms, contributor agreements, employment or contractor agreements, publishing agreements, event rules, platform terms and project-specific confidentiality notices. A separately signed agreement takes priority where it expressly governs the same confidential information.

This page is a general NDA template and should be reviewed and adapted by qualified legal counsel before it is used as a binding confidentiality agreement, particularly where signatures, specific parties, governing law, duration or project-specific remedies are required.
