Why Voidshard

We started Voidshard to make ambitious games without losing the directness, trust and speed of a focused team. We believe the best work happens when disciplines collaborate early and everyone can see how their contribution reaches the player.

How we work

Small cross-functional groups own features from prototype to polish. We favour playable proof over presentation, honest feedback over ceremony, and sustainable progress over crunch.

Our home

Liverpool is creative, independent and globally connected. Our studio is planned as a flexible home base for local talent, with hybrid working designed into the company from the beginning.
