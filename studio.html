Voidshard is a focused Unreal studio for people who want to stay close to the work and close to the game.

Why Voidshard

We started Voidshard to make ambitious games without building a maze around the people making them. The team stays small enough for ideas to travel quickly, for feedback to be honest and for everyone to see their fingerprints on the final game.

How we work

We prototype before we posture. Small cross-functional groups take ideas from rough greyboxes to polished features, with playable proof carrying more weight than slides, titles or ceremony.

Our home

Liverpool is inventive, independent and full of game-making history. We want Voidshard to feel like it belongs here: direct, creative, welcoming and willing to do things a little differently.
